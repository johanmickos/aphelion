APHELION  ·  DIRECTION 08  ·  THE SCORING CONSTITUTION
THE FIELD PAYS FOR PROGRESS.
SKILL SETS THE PRICE.
THE BANK REMEMBERS.
Every number in the game now answers to one law: points are earned by moving through the world, and multiplied by how well you moved. Nothing pays for waiting, nothing pays twice, and every multiplier is a fact the player already saw drawn on an instrument before it touched the score. If a scoring rule can't point at the pixel that announced it, the rule is wrong.
THE FIVE AXIOMS
1 · Progress is the only base currency. Metres climbed while engaged. Not time, not kills, not combos of combos.
2 · Skill only multiplies. Accuracy, risk, consistency, engagement — none of them mint points; they price the metres.
3 · Coasting is unpaid, never punished. Disengaged metres earn ×0. No decay, no drain.
4 · Points exist in two states. Carried (at stake, glowing) or banked (safe, dim). Death takes only what's carried… in most modes — see the matrix.
5 · Every multiplier has a pixel. Tier = the dot. Band = the motes. Streak = the ×N. Chain = the craft's bloom. No invisible math.
THE EARN → CASH → BANK LOOP — SCORING FOLLOWS THE SWING
The carry is visible on the craft. Carried points ride as the brightness of the trail (E-channel, like everything at stake); banked points are the dim BANK chip. The player can always see what death would cost, without reading a number.
The grade prices the whole carry, not just the moment. A PERFECT doesn't award a flat bonus — it doubles everything you carried through that orbit. High carries into hard windows are the game's core bet, and the compass arc width is the posted odds.
Chain is engagement, not accuracy — it counts consecutive engaged swings (no coasting gap over one full rung, 25m), gates the craft's bloom, and adds its flat +10%/link inside the carry accrual. Streaks (Direction 06) are accuracy and live in the cash step. Two systems, two pixels, no overlap.

Multipliers multiply — they never add to each other — so the theoretical max per swing is legible: 2.0 × 3.0 × 1.5 = ×9 over base. The +445-style floating gain of the old build is gone; the callout is the receipt, and the BANK tick is its confirmation.
WHAT DELIBERATELY EARNS NOTHING
Velocity — the headline number and worth zero points. It's the *cause* of scoring (more speed → more metres per swing → bigger carries), never scored itself. That's what keeps it a fun number instead of a grind meter.
Orbiting — holding an orbit earns nothing per lap. Altitude while orbiting ≈ 0, so the formula already says so; no special rule needed. Waiting is priced at exactly its altitude value: none.
Near-misses, grazes, style — no invisible judges. If the game can't point at the instrument that graded it, it doesn't grade it.
Survival time — never. Time pressure comes from the field escalating (Infinite) or the clock being the score (Corridors), not from a per-second trickle.
Design smell this prevents: "engagement rewards" that pay players for not playing well. Aphelion pays for exactly one thing — skilled forward motion — priced four ways.
