APHELION  ·  DIRECTION 01  ·  TOKEN SYSTEM + PLANET COMPASS
Brightness is commitment.
Colour is identity.
Pink is risk.
The point of view: Aphelion is an instrument you fly. Everything luminous on screen is either the player's stored energy or a fact about the future — nothing glows as decoration. The signature element, the thing a screenshot is recognised by, is the compass: a white-hot craft inside a set of coloured windows. Everything around it stays disciplined so the compass can spend the boldness.
WHAT THE DEFAULT WOULD HAVE BEEN — REJECTED
Acid accent on near-black → palette derived from the aurora's violet–pink and a stellar identity band.
Monospace HUD → arcade display face + grotesk data face. This is a game, not a simulator.
Radial glow bursts → every streak falls in parallel. Side-on, no vanishing point, ever.
Score-first hierarchy → velocity is the headline; score is bookkeeping.
THE OPEN CALL — RESOLVED
Hue is identity.
Quality is energy.
Identity needs many distinguishable values and must extend — every adventure region adds body types. Hue is the only channel with enough levels.
Quality is ordinal. Luminance, bloom radius and motion natively mean "more". The ship already obeys this rule: its glow builds with the chain.
They coexist because they never share an element: an arc keeps its hue forever and heats in place. Nothing on screen ever changes hue to mean "better".
Award tiers inherit it: DEADEYE and CLEAN differ by bloom and scale, never by colour.
PALETTE — EIGHT NAMES, EIGHT MEANINGS · THE CANONICAL RECORD
VOID #0A0814
The sky. Violet-black, never pure. True #000 exists only in the anomaly's cloud gaps — so the anomaly reads deeper than ordinary space.
DUSK #6C64A6
Structure. Rings at rest, field lines, spent bodies, secondary data. DUSK never glows — it is the unlit state of everything.
AURORA #9D6BFF
Strange: anomaly sky, black holes, farewell rings. Purple means the rules are different here — never "good," never identity. (Dir. 04)
ION #FF5FA2
Risk and tension: boundary gradient, deadline, low fuel. Pink is never decoration — if it's pink, it can cost you the bank.
CORE #FFF4E0
The player. Craft, trail, release hand, TRUE callouts. The brightest value in the game — nothing else may reach it.
LUMEN #7FE0A8
Quality, mid tier: SHARP callouts — type only. One world monopoly: the finish system (carpet, checkered line). Green = sanctuary, ION's mirror. (Dir. 06, 12)
SOLAR #FFC94A
Quality, top tier: PERFECT callouts and their ×N. Type only, same law as LUMEN. (Dir. 06)
INK #EDEAF7
Utility text at full strength: data, labels, chips. Never blooms — text light belongs to the quality colours.
The identity band is a rule, not a list: planet hues are generated at oklch(0.72 0.13 H), H stepped ≥50° between neighbours, excluding the violet–pink band (strange + risk: AURORA, ION) and the quality bands (LUMEN green, SOLAR gold). Exemplar slots: ember 55° · teal 170° · azure 215° · blue 265° — greens sit at teal, ≥20° clear of LUMEN; blues stop at 265°, short of AURORA's violet. Fixed lightness and chroma keep every identity equally loud; the rule extends to every region's new bodies. One exception, by decree: black holes wear AURORA.

EMISSION — FOUR TIERS, NO EXCEPTIONS
E0 STRUCTURE — no bloom. Rings at rest, field lines, spent bodies.
E1 LIT — 6px bloom @ 35%. Active windows, planet limbs, labels.
E2 HOT — 18px bloom @ 60% + white core. Craft baseline, a window under live aim, the perfect dot when matched.
E3 FLASH — 48px, additive, 400ms decay. Release, capture, award. Only one E3 alive at a time.
Bloom radius is the quality channel. Chain level adds +4px per link to the craft's E2 — a hot run is visibly hotter, in radius, never in hue.
MOTION — WHAT "PUNCHY" MEANS HERE
ENTER
120ms, overshoot cubic-bezier(.2, 1.6, .3, 1), from 92% scale. Things arrive; they don't fade in.
DECAY
420ms exponential. Nothing persists past 600ms except the trail.
HITSTOP
70ms world freeze at release and capture. The pause is the punch.
KICK
Camera 6px along the exit tangent, home in 180ms. Along the tangent only — never a shake.
RULE
Attack ≤ 2 frames; decay ≥ 10× attack. All streaks parallel to velocity — nothing ever radiates from a point.
THE PLANET COMPASS
same information as today, arcade presentation — and it now grades your aim before you release
1
The craft is the brightest object on screen, always. CORE white, E2 baseline; each chain link adds bloom radius — try the chain tweak. It carries no identity hue: the player is not a target.
2
Live aim quality, on the release hand. The hand is the prototype's play-tested radius through the craft — kept. The nose says where (it always points along the tangent it will depart on); the hand says when. The segment to the ghost dot thickens and brightens as the gap closes; the window under the hand heats from E1 to E2. Luminance only — nothing changes hue. The award word after release confirms what the player already saw.
3
Ghost dots ride the hand — one per ring crossing, the active ring's brightest. The visible gap between ghost and perfect dot is the grade — a fact drawn on the geometry, never a command.
4
The arc carries the rarity ladder. Full arc = CLEAN, bright inner zone = SHAVED, the dot = DEADEYE. Arc length still encodes difficulty; now it also names its own rewards.
5
Labels live at their arc's tip, on their own radius. They cannot collide across rings because they never share one; if two tips come within 12°, the outer label slides along its own ring until clear. No more P11P9.P3894510k.
6
The held body is spent. On capture, a planet drops to DUSK structure. Colour belongs only to what still pulls — so the compass answers "where next?" before the eye asks.
7
Rings at rest are E0 structure. Only windows carry colour. That's why four-plus targets stay readable: the quiet parts are actually quiet.
8
The thumb line. During a hold, nothing below the lower third needs reading — the instrument lives in the upper two-thirds, above the thumb, around where the eye already is.
THE COMPASS THROUGH ONE SWING — INCLUDING BEFORE THE ORBIT EXISTS
1 · PRESS
The grab line: holding bends a filament to the body that pulls hardest, in its identity hue, and its orbit fades in. The press is taught by the same instrument that grades the release.
2 · ORBIT
Captured. Windows drawn at E1, aim cold, line thin. The instrument is at rest — tension hasn't started.
3 · CLOSING
The gap is the grade. Ghost dot approaches the perfect dot; the line thickens; the window heats toward E2.
4 · MATCHED
Ghost and perfect dot merge white-hot. The player knows it's a DEADEYE before letting go — the instrument states the fact; the release is still theirs.
5 · RELEASE
E3 flash, 70ms hitstop, exit streak along the tangent. The taken arc stays lit and decays behind you; the unused rings die instantly. The compass resolves — it doesn't vanish.
THE ARC, UNROLLED — 45 AWARD WORDS BECOME 3
One word per tier, so every word repeats often enough to become a signal. The word lands at the release point on the arc — not in a band at the top of the screen — then rides the exit tangent and decays in 420ms. Tiers differ by type scale and bloom, never hue. Queueing is structural: one release, one word, and the E3 rule means two can't fight.
