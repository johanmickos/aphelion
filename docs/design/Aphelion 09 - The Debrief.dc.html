THE TALLY — DOT LEADERS, TICKED IN
Three rows, dot leaders, values right-flush in 800 weight — the arcade high-score table's grammar. Rows land one per ~120ms with a tick; numbers slam in whole (arcade boards count, they never fade). The headline number is the mode's verdict: SOLAR for what you kept (Infinite), DUSK for what the field kept (Daily). The debrief line keeps its grammar: cause + place + one number, machine-assembled, one dry pink clause.
THE BLINK — ONE INPUT, EVEN HERE
No buttons on the card. Tap anywhere = the blinking verb; hold = the quiet one under it. Press, hold, release survives as the entire interface — menus included. The blink runs at 1Hz on a steps() cadence (on 60%, off 40%): the insert-coin rhythm, the oldest "your move" signal in the arcade. Nothing else on the card ever animates after the tally lands; the blink owns the silence.
