APHELION  ·  DIRECTION 12  ·  THE FINISH LINE
THE CARPET
TAKES YOU HOME.
The finish is the boundary's mirror. The pink edge is the end that kills; the green line is the end that saves — same grammar, band + line + gradient, opposite verdict. Half a screen below the checkered line begins the safety carpet: cross into it and the run is mathematically over — gravity lets go, the carpet lift blends your velocity into its own, opens the throttle halfway up, and throws you up past the top of the screen. FINISH! bubbles at the line like every other callout. The game's most dangerous instrument gets a twin that only ever says yes.
THE LAW AMENDMENT — GREEN IS SANCTUARY
LUMEN gains one world monopoly: the finish system. Carpet, chevrons, checkered line, carpet dots — nothing else in the world may wear green, exactly as nothing but risk wears pink. Recorded in the 01 palette.
Why it doesn't collide with SHARP: the two greens agree — green means good news, in type and in terrain. Confusion needs contradiction; there is none.
The checker is the game's only pattern fill. Everything else is line and glow. The checkered flag is the one universal racing glyph — spending our single texture on it makes the finish unmistakable at any speed.
One input, honored by removing it: the carpet is the game's only no-input zone. The run ended when you earned the carpet; the lift is the bow, not a level.
THE SEQUENCE — ~2.2s FROM CARPET EDGE TO GONE
CARPET EDGE
Gravity lets go — your last swing doesn't. The trajectory and velocity of the final release carry into the carpet: swing dead straight and you ride the centre; fling hard left and you travel far left while the lift gently trues you up to a left-side finish. The carpet corrects, it never erases — the finish position is the last release's signature. Compass and gauges die instantly (nothing left to decide); the velocity readout stays for the show.
THE LIFT · 0–50%
Vertical speed blends toward carpet speed over ~400ms — a smooth catch, never a jerk. The chunky chevron mat brightens row by row under the craft, a retro speed-up strip in strict parallel. Carpet dots are scattered loose across the carpet, visible on approach — your line sweeps up whichever it happens to cross, +150 flat each. Pocket change outside the wager: no tiers, no multipliers, no aiming. Found money, not earned money.
THE SURGE · 50%
Halfway up the carpet the lift opens the throttle — early enough that most of the ride is the fast part: velocity readout climbs, dust streaks stretch, the mat blurs. The game spends its speed vocabulary on you one last time.
THE CROSSING
No hitstop — the finish never interrupts momentum; that grammar belongs to decisions, and there is nothing left to decide. The checker flashes once (E3, the only green E3 in the game), FINISH! bubbles up at the crossing point — wherever your lane ended up — and the craft leaves the top of the screen at full song.
THE EMPTY SKY
The camera does not follow. It holds on the checkered line cooling and the trail fading for ~800ms — the same "let it go" the release taught — then the results card (Direction 10) snaps in over the empty field. Victory is the screen the craft is no longer on.
LIVE — THE CARPET LIFT, LOOPED
The loop alternates three final swings — straight, hard left, hard right — and the finish lane follows each one. Tweak liftCatch, surge, and lane correction.
