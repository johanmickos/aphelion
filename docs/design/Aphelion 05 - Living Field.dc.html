YOU CLIMB THROUGH
A MEDIUM, NOT PAST
A BACKDROP.
The Geometry Wars grid is loved for a quality, not a shape: a continuous medium that bends around mass and reacts to the player. The shape can't come with us — a grid is a floor plane. The quality can. Aphelion's field is made of altitude rungs: horizontal strata hung across the world every 25m, perpendicular to travel — a ladder, not a floor. They bow toward every mass, part around the craft, and sweep past as you climb, so speed, gravity, and your own passage are all visible in the one system.
THE TRIBUTE, EARNED — WHAT TRANSLATES, WHAT DIES
Mesh carpet under the world → rungs across it. Horizontal lines perpendicular to travel imply no floor and no horizon.
Mesh deformation → gravity bow. Rungs sag toward every body — real equipotential behaviour, and the tide (Direction 04) is its close-up.
Ripples radiating from action → the wake. Rungs part around the craft and relax behind it. Nothing radiates; radiation implies a point of view.
Parallax star layers → refused entirely. Layers at different speeds are implied depth. Everything in the field moves at world speed; dust varies in brightness, never in velocity.
LIVE — THE FIELD IN MOTION
Rungs sweep past as you climb — each one a real altitude, every 5th carrying its address (the same numbers the planets wear). They bow toward the planet, part around the craft, and dust falls in strict parallel at world speed. Tweak gravityBend, wake, and climbSpeed.
WHAT THE RUNGS DO — THREE JOBS, ONE SYSTEM
SPEED, FELT
The trail says where you've been; the rungs say how fast the world is passing. At high velocity they streak; hanging at an orbit's apex they barely drift. The dotted-breadcrumb problem dies here — motion is read from the world, not sampled from the path.
GRAVITY, DRAWN
Every body bows the rungs toward itself, radius scaled by mass — the field states which planets pull hardest before you ever press. Heavy bodies announce themselves at a distance; the tide is this same statement at the rim.
ALTITUDE, ADDRESSED
Every 5th rung carries its altitude number, utility-face, E0-quiet. The field is a ruler you climb: banked score has a visible geography, "died at 23" has a place, and the results sheet's route recap maps straight back onto the world. And the rule generalizes: rungs are level sets of progress, perpendicular to intended travel — horizontal in the vertical field, radial ticks on a ring course. Authored corridors of any shape inherit the whole field grammar unchanged.
THE STACK — EVERYTHING AT WORLD SPEED
SKY
VOID, with a slow altitude ramp: the violet-black warms almost imperceptibly toward AURORA as an anomaly approaches — weather on the horizon, never spent early. Outside that, the sky changes only by region.

Sparse motes, strictly parallel fall, brightness varied (α .1–.3), velocity identical to the rungs. Density rises gently with chain level — a hot run flies through a livelier field.

One flat world, five kinds of light. Depth cues — parallax, scale drift, blur-by-distance — are banned in all five. The anomaly keeps its crown: it is the only event allowed to repaint the sky, and the baseline's restraint is what keeps it rare.
