APHELION  ·  DIRECTION 10  ·  THE RESULTS SHEET — VICTORY AS A SHARE ARTIFACT
THE ROUTE IS THE TROPHY.
A score is a claim; a route is a proof. The results sheet's hero is the run itself, drawn as a light-line through the whole field — every grab, every PERFECT, every fire-band dip legible in one glance, unique as a fingerprint. Same glass-card language as the debrief (Direction 09), composed to be screenshotted: everything inside a 4:5 safe frame, wordmark on the glass, no UI chrome to crop out. And because the game is deterministic, the sheet carries a recipe line — seed + input log as a short code — so a shared run isn't a picture of a claim, it's a replayable fact.
WHAT MAKES IT SHAREABLE — FOUR DECISIONS
The route reads without a legend. White line, gold dots, pink dips: a stranger sees skill shape before they know the game. That's what makes a feed stop.
One number rules the card. Score (Daily/Infinite) or time (Corridors). Everything else is dot-leader small print.
The day is named on the glass. DAY 214 + field name — so two screenshots side by side are instantly the same contest, and tomorrow's is instantly a new one.
The recipe replaces the video. APH-214-KX7Q pastes into the game and replays the run as a ghost. Proof, bragging, and a tutorial in fourteen characters.
THE SHEET — DAILY TOPPED OUT · FIELD ALIVE BEHIND THE GLASS · 4:5 SAFE FRAME MARKED
In-app, TAP TO SHARE blinks below the glass (insert-coin cadence, per 09); the screenshot crops to exactly what's shown — nothing above or below the card matters.
THE ROUTE LANGUAGE — THE WHOLE SYSTEM, MINIATURIZED
The route map is the game's vocabulary at 1:400 scale, no legend needed because every mark keeps its in-game meaning: white line = the flight; gold dots = PERFECTs at the exact planets that earned them; green ticks = SHARPs; pink walls and tinted band = how deep into fire the line dared to live; violet stratum = the anomaly, so the wavering of the line inside it is visible; DUSK circles = spent bodies, the run's rosary. A cautious run is a straight pale line up the middle. A great run hugs the right wall and glitters. Skill has a silhouette — that's the trophy.
THE RECIPE LINE
Seed + compressed input log, checksummed: APH-214-KX7Q. Pasting it into the game replays the run as a CORE-white ghost over your own attempt. It makes every share verifiable (the leaderboard accepts nothing else), turns bragging into teaching, and costs fourteen characters. The code renders in INK at 800 — it's a fact, not a link.
MODE VARIANTS — SAME CARD, ONE SWAP
Infinite: headline = banked total; route map scrolls to show the final 500m with an altitude badge. Corridors: headline = time in Anton (1:42.8), route drawn on the course shape — the donut's ring with your line lapping it is the single best share image the game can produce. Zen: no headline at all; the route alone, full-bleed. The subtraction mode even subtracts from the trophy.
TOPPED OUT — WHAT DAILY VICTORY MEANS
The Daily has a top (40 bodies), so finishing is an event: the top-out replaces the debrief entirely — the craft exits the field's last rung, the camera lets it go, and the card snaps in on the empty sky it left. Line one: CLEARED THE FIELD · 4 PERFECT · NOTHING LEFT TO TAKE. The wager framing pays off here too: victory is the field failing to keep your money.
