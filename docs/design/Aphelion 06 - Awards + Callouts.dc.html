POINTS FOR THE MAKE.
WORDS FOR THE MASTERY.
A word that never repeats never becomes a signal — and a word for "merely made it" devalues every word above it. So the baseline tier speaks in points alone, and the vocabulary is exactly three, on the rarity ladder players already know: TRUE in white, SHARP in green, PERFECT in gold. Streaks escalate by counting — PERFECT ×4, not a synonym. The word, its points, and its colour arrive as one unit at the release point: pop, linger, and fall behind as you fly on.
RETIRED, AND WHY
CLEAN · SHAVED · DEADEYE (rev 1) → read as grooming and slang, not marksmanship. Words must survive being said aloud by a twelve-year-old and a broadcaster alike.
SURGICAL, IMMACULATE, FLAWLESS… → absorbed into PERFECT ×N. Synonyms flatter; counters testify.
Event words (anomaly entry, boundary graze…) → deleted. The aurora is the callout; a word on a spectacle is a caption on a firework.
Heavy display face in motion → retired from callouts. Anton keeps the masthead; moving text belongs to the utility face, bold, tracked.
THE LADDER — ZONES OF THE WINDOW, PRICED AND COLOURED
Zones scale with the window, so difficulty prices the words automatically — a PERFECT on a needle-thin arc is a different feat than on a barn door, and the arc's width already said so.
A miss gets silence. Outside the window: no word, no points, no sting. The capture you didn't make is the feedback. Nothing is confiscated; rewards are withheld.
STREAK RULES — PINNED
Per-word, broken only downward. PERFECT ×N counts consecutive PERFECTs; any lesser result resets it. A PERFECT doesn't break a SHARP streak — it upgrades it, opening a PERFECT ×1. No timer: coasting between grabs can't expire a streak — only a graded release or death changes it. Expiry-by-clock would punish route-reading.
×N appears at the second occurrence — a repeat within the linger merges in place (counter ticks, smaller re-pop). Each step adds +10% to the tier multiplier, capped at 5 steps: enough to chase, not enough to warp the field's own pricing. Longest PERFECT streak is a headline stat on the results sheet.
LIVE — POP, LINGER, FALL BEHIND
Word over points, one unit, in tier colour: 120ms pop upward with overshoot, then anchored to the world — it drifts past as you climb and fades. PERFECTs merge to ×N; a new callout snaps the old to its decay tail. Tweak cadence and drift.
CALLOUT ANATOMY — TYPE, COLOUR, MOTION
Type — utility face, 800 caps, tracked 0.1em. Moving text needs open counters, not display weight; Anton stays on the masthead.
Unit — word over points, born at the release point, 8–30px off the dot. Score meets attention where attention already is.
Motion — 120ms pop upward, one overshoot, then world-anchored: it lingers ~1.2s and falls behind at world speed. The pop buys the glance; leaving it behind sells the speed.
The callout ladder is white → green → gold — the rarity convention players arrive knowing. Violet is deliberately absent: it belongs to the anomaly and to black holes — purple means strange, never good. LUMEN #7FE0A8 and SOLAR #FFC94A join the palette with one law: quality colours live only in type — no planet, ring, or gauge ever wears them, no callout ever wears an identity hue, and the identity band excludes both (green identities shift toward jade-cyan, clear of LUMEN). Geometry and typography never share the colour channel, so hue-as-identity survives intact — and the word–score–colour fusion the prototype proved satisfying is kept whole.
THE PINNED CEILING — A SYSTEMS NOTE
The multiplier binds ~22s in and never moves again — a dead number wearing live paint. Recommendation: once the ceiling binds, over-ceiling links convert to visible bank sparks — a mote of light fires from the craft to the BANK chip and the number ticks on arrival. The channel stays alive, the reward is honest, and the chip earns its home by being the destination of things worth watching.
Chain milestones (×5, ×10, ×15) get a masthead pulse and one bloom step on the craft — no word. The number is the callout; vocabulary is reserved for releases.
