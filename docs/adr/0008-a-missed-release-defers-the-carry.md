# A missed release defers the carry rather than destroying it

Direction 06 says a miss gets silence: "no word, no points, no sting. Nothing is
confiscated; rewards are withheld." Direction 08 says the carry is cashed at release and
priced by tier. Read together, a release outside the window prices at ×0 and the metres
climbed during that orbit evaporate — which is confiscation, and it breaks both
`VISION.md`'s fifth pillar and the constitution's own third axiom.

So an out-of-window release **does not cash at all**. The carry is not lost and not paid;
it rides into the next swing and cashes when a graded release finally earns it. A bad
release becomes a debt rather than a loss.

**Consequence**: carry is not bounded by one swing, so it can accumulate across several
missed releases and cash enormously on one good one. That is intended — it is the same
"withholding, never confiscating" pressure the fifth pillar describes, and it gives a
struggling run a reason to keep flying. It does mean the carry display has to stay
legible at values a single swing could never reach, and that death still takes the whole
carry, which is what keeps the deferral a stake rather than a free ride.
