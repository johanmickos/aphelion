# Fuel is returned by skill, and powerups may never pay points

Fuel is the currency of the save: Direction 03 draws the deadline window at its true size
but lights only the fraction the tank can afford, and Direction 07 drains the halo during
the carve. Nothing in the design says what refills it, and the choice decides what kind
of play the game rewards.

Fuel is returned **in proportion to the tier of a release**, not in proportion to the
points cashed. Points scale with metres climbed, so paying fuel per point would refuel
longest on the longest orbits — rewarding the slow, coasting-adjacent play the economy
deliberately leaves unpaid. Tier-proportional fuel says "you flew well", which is the same
sentence the compass says, so the two instruments agree. Orbiting an anomaly trickles fuel,
and surviving a burn refunds part of what the burn cost, so a well-flown dive is nearly
free and a panicked one is expensive.

Powerups join the same law from the other side: **they may pay fuel and time, never points
and never multipliers.** A powerup is something the field gives you, not something you
earned by flying well, so any powerup that touched the score would mint points and repeal
the constitution's second axiom. Fuel-and-time keeps them a clean, separable system — the
field's generosity, as against the economy's wage.
