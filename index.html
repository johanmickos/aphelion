PHASE RATE
how fast the settled ship sweeps its orbit · 1.0 = physically real speed · >1 = faster around the SAME orbit (decoupled)
TIGHTEN
final roundness of the settled orbit · 1 = rounds to a circle after ~1 loop · lower = keeps a residual oval
SETTLE T
seconds to ease from the natural ellipse into the tightened, phase-swept orbit
PHASE RAMP
how quickly the phase rate reaches its target after the freeze (lower = gentler seam)
CIRC COST
fuel/sec burned while circularizing · full settle costs this × settle-time · the slingshot/whip itself is always free
FLYBY COST
fuel/sec burned while braking a too-fast flyby into a capture
RECOVERY
fuel/sec replenished when not spending · lower = slower refill between captures
GAUGE BOB
how much the compass ring bobs with the orbit · 0 = still ring · 1 = full peri/apo pump
